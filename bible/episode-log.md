## Idea bank (unused concepts from brainstorms)
- **The Strip-Bark Pine.** A bristlecone pine survives millennia by letting whole sections of itself die, so a thin strip of bark keeps the rest alive. *What you let go of is how you stay.*
- **The Eel Who Went Home.** European eels cross the Atlantic to the Sargasso Sea to spawn, and no one has ever watched them do it.
- **The Pearl's Confession.** Natural pearls rarely form around sand; usually it's a parasite or a wound.
- **The Stone the Ice Forgot.** A glacial erratic (e.g. Okotoks "Big Rock") carried hundreds of km and left behind.
- **The Firefly Out of Time.** Synchronous fireflies (*Photinus carolinus*). Harmony without a conductor.
